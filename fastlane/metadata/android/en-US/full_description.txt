SMS2TG forwards incoming SMS messages from your Android phone to a private Telegram chat using a Telegram bot that you create and control. No third‑party servers are involved — the only external host your device talks to is api.telegram.org.

<b>Features</b>

• Background‑first. A foreground service keeps the SMS receiver alive even when the app is closed, including on aggressive OEM skins (MIUI/HyperOS, ColorOS, EMUI).
• Private by design. The bot token is stored in Android Keystore via EncryptedSharedPreferences and never leaves your device.
• Reliable delivery. Outgoing messages are handed to WorkManager with exponential backoff; network drops and transient Telegram errors retry automatically.
• Guided setup. A 4‑step wizard walks you through BotFather → token → chat binding → test message.
• Diagnostics built in. A status card surfaces permission state, last SMS seen, last delivery, last error.
• Customizable template. MarkdownV2 template with {from} / {body} placeholders and optional SIM slot for dual‑SIM.
• OEM hints. On Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO devices, a dedicated button opens MIUI's autostart settings.

<b>No tracking</b>

SMS2TG contains no analytics, no crash reporters and no advertising SDKs. The only network destination is api.telegram.org.
